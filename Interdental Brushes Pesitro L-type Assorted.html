Description

Interdental Brushes Assorted Brush sizes in the pack: 0.4 mm, 0.5 mm, 0.6 mm, 0.7 mm, 0.8 mm, 1.1 mm.
You can choose the size that best fits your individual interdental spaces.
An easy and effective way to prevent cavities, gingivitis, and periodontitis.
Designed for daily cleaning of interdental spaces. Pack contains 6 brushes.

Directions for Use:

Moisten the bristles of the interdental brush with water. While holding the brush at a 90-degree angle to the surface of your teeth, gently insert it into the interdental space until the bristles appear on the other side.The brush wire should slide in smoothly without force—if resistance is felt, choose a different size or model.
