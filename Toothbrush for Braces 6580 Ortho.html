Toothbrush for braces 6580 Ortho

Description

• PESITRO UltraClean Ultra Soft Ortho 6580 is a soft toothbrush with a U-shaped indentation, specially designed for gentle care during orthodontic treatment. The brush is intended for daily cleaning of braces and teeth. It contains 6,580 soft Ultra-X bristles — ultra-fine polyester fibers with a diameter of 0.10 mm. It ensures effective and non-traumatic plaque removal.

The advantage of the Pesitro 6580 Ortho toothbrush:

• The brush features an increased number of ultra-fine bristles, with a diameter of 0.10 mm.

• The compact brush head design prevents injury to soft tissues.

• The ergonomic handle allows you to hold the toothbrush at the correct angle to the cleaning surface.

• Available in eight vibrant colors.
