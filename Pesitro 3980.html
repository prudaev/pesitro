UltraClean Super Soft

Description

The PESITRO 3980 UltraClean Super Soft toothbrush is equipped with 3,980 ultra-fine polyester fiber bristles, each measuring just 0.10 mm in thickness. These soft bristles ensure a gentle yet thorough cleaning experience, ideal for people with sensitive teeth and gums. The polyester material absorbs minimal moisture, helping to reduce bacterial growth on the brush head.

Advantages of PESITRO toothbrushes:

• Ultra-soft, rounded bristle tips provide effective plaque removal while minimizing the risk of gum irritation or bleeding.

• Ergonomically designed handle made from safe, eco-friendly plastic — hypoallergenic and comfortable to hold.

• Dentists recommend replacing your toothbrush every 2 months and after any infectious illness.

• Optimal brushing time: 2–3 minutes for maximum oral hygiene.

• All PESITRO products are certified and comply with international safety and quality standards.
